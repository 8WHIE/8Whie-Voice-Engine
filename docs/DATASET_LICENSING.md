## 1. Ethical Dataset Compliance Policy
8WHIE operates under a strict legal standard:
- No copyrighted audio scraped without permission.
- No proprietary audio samples from commercial services.
- No datasets containing non-commercial (NC) clauses for commercial deployments.

## 3. Contributor & Speaker Consent Standards
Any individual contributing audio to 8WHIE VoiceForge must sign an explicit biometric speech consent release granting commercial synthesis rights.
